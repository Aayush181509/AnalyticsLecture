Business case previews

These are proposed case themes, not released assignments. We will combine suitable Tableau sample datasets with the existing synthetic-data strategy. Meridian Commerce remains a proposed fictional simulation; NPR/calendar2025 conventions apply only to that simulation. Other datasets retain their own context, units and dates. Individual files have not yet been released for class.

Domain Stakeholder decision Analytical focus Mistake to investigate
Retail/e-commerce Where should sales and margin improvement efforts focus? Grain, distinct orders, AOV, margin, discounts, customers, trends and returns. Treating order lines as orders.
Finance Which departments need a spending review? Budget vs actual, periods, reconciliation and cost favorability. Repeating a monthly budget for every transaction.
Operations Where should delivery reliability improve? Promise dates, overdue open shipments, distributions, median and mean. Dropping undelivered shipments from service measurement.
Marketing Which campaigns need closer evaluation before budget moves? Weighted funnel rates, cost per conversion, ROAS and attribution limits. Averaging percentages with different denominators.
HR How is the workforce changing, and which comparisons are fair? Historical headcount, turnover, cohorts, tenure and recruitment timelines. Dividing past exits by current headcount.

Each released case will include a stakeholder brief, dataset dictionary, explicit metrics, guided example, independent exercises, a debugging task, a recommendation challenge, validation checkpoints and an optional pandas connection. Instructor answers will be supplied separately.

Across departments, explain differences in grain, period and units before combining evidence. Marketing-attributed revenue is a view of retail revenue, not an additional source of company revenue. A recommendation should consider trade-offs rather than add incompatible KPIs together.

Additional practice choices

We will also draw on sports, technology, government and other catalog topics. Proposed choices include World Cup match comparisons, mobile operating-system trends, start-up funding, public construction spending, and home-sales trends. Science, education and entertainment offer further options. These are transfer exercises for the same analytical process, with source-specific definitions and checks. They fit existing practice or optional extensions; the five core cases remain the portfolio foundation.

Catalog listing alone does not establish the row grain, supported calculations or redistribution terms. Each lesson will provide the exact reviewed file and dictionary or direct-source instructions before you use it.

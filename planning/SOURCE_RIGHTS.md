## Statcounter: explicit license and meaningful denominator

The [Statcounter Global Stats FAQ](https://gs.statcounter.com/faq) states that its work is under **Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0 Unported** and requests linked credit wherever its statistics, data or graphs are used. Its methodology describes page-view activity across its participating websites, not device sales or unique people, and says it does not impose country weighting. These are provider-wide definitions to check against the historical CSV, not proof that today's sample sizes or methodology apply unchanged to 2008–2014.

The [license deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) allows sharing and adaptation, including commercial use, subject to attribution and the stated terms. It describes ShareAlike for adaptations and forbids added restrictions; it also notes other rights and gives no warranties. Record creator/title if supplied, link to source and license, and describe course transformations. Do not change the source license to the course's eventual material license. Whether a specific workbook or combined artifact constitutes an adaptation is not settled by this audit; keep source-derived artifacts distinguishable and review their distribution notices.

**Recommended attribution template, to complete after file audit:** “Mobile OS Usage — Statcounter Global Stats; [actual verified geography/period]; source [original chart/export link]; mirrored by Tableau; CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported; course preparation: [listed changes]; accessed 2026-10-12.” Include linked provider/source/license references beside the download and visualization, not only in an internal manifest.

The [catalog's historical Statcounter link](https://gs.statcounter.com/#mobile_os-ww-monthly-200812-201412) was opened, but text-only browsing rendered a default contemporary browser chart and a JavaScript requirement, not the requested historical mobile-OS view. Therefore the URL fragment is not treated as verification of the CSV's exact geography, interval, values or categories. Lead file inspection must confirm these before freezing metrics. Use “usage share” with the audited population; do not describe it as share of owners or sales.

## Census: qualified public-access statement and construction definitions

The official [Research Transparency and Public Access policy, p. 9](https://www2.census.gov/foia/ds_policies/ds027.pdf) states that employee-created Census data/works generally lack copyright protection **within the United States**, while noting foreign-country protection and contractor/assigned-work possibilities. This is not an observed worldwide public-domain declaration for the exact Enigma mirror. The course's Nepal context and the third-party mirror are reasons to preserve that qualification rather than invent a universal license.

[Census citation guidance](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/citation.html) asks users to credit the original Census data and makes analysts responsible for their derived conclusions. Recommended citation components include agency, product/table/file, vintage, URL and access date. For the mirror, retain Enigma/Tableau as the distribution/preparation chain as well as Census as the stated original source; no Census endorsement of course conclusions should be implied.

The [construction definitions](https://www.census.gov/construction/c30/definitions.html) define value put in place as work installed/erected during a period. It includes items such as materials, labor, contractor profit and certain project costs; ownership during construction distinguishes private from government-owned work. It is not inherently a department budget-versus-actual ledger, and “government” is not an appropriate label for every construction row.

The official [construction methodology](https://www.census.gov/construction/c30/methodology.html) describes seasonal adjustment and revision/benchmarking processes. Consequently, the course must audit whether this mirrored series is adjusted or unadjusted, an annualized rate or period total, its units, vintage and overlapping categories before choosing aggregation. This research did not identify those properties for the CSV. No rate-to-amount conversion or historical comparability claim is approved here. Official [historical data tables](https://www.census.gov/construction/c30/historical_data.html) provide a possible original-source comparison path; no table was downloaded in this task.

**Unresolved mirror questions:** the exact upstream Enigma dataset/version and its notices, whether columns/values were transformed, and the applicable mirror/compilation terms. No explicit Enigma redistribution terms for this file were found in the bounded research. A failed open of an assumed Census copyright-page URL and a construction FAQ URL supplies no permission evidence; the successfully read policy above is the recorded authority.
